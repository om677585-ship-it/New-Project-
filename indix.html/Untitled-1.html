How to Use

General usage guidance — always follow the instructions on your product pack

  • Antox-D: Mix half a teaspoon of Antox-D into 100ml water, or take 5ml as directed. Take once on an empty stomach in the morning, and once about an hour before dinner.
  • Antox Amrut 51: Take 10ml on an empty stomach every morning.
  • Antox-PN Powder: Mix 10g (2 teaspoons) in a cup of warm milk, once daily.
  • Antox-PN Oil: Apply and gently massage on the affected area as needed.
  • For best results, most products are recommended for at least 3 months of regular use, along with a suitable diet.
